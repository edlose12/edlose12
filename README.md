# Hi, I'm Edwin 👋

Founder of **[Markible](https://markible.it)**, a software company building AI-first SaaS products.
Based in Rome, Italy.

The idea behind all of them: **the AI does the heavy lifting, people keep the judgment.**

## What I'm building

- **MarketingJot**: an AI-first workspace for marketing managers. The AI delivers a ready-made plan; the manager orchestrates, refines and organizes it.
- **[Scenio](https://scenio.it)**: business plans and scenarios without spreadsheets, for Italian startups and SMEs. Guided narrative with AI as co-author, plus a driver-based financial model (P&L, cash flow, balance sheet) and side-by-side scenario comparison.
- **BloomGO**: a job-monitoring platform for young people (18–30). An agent checks the careers pages you choose every day and flags new openings, then helps you apply with tailored CVs and cover letters.
